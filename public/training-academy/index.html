The route

Learning certification and competence are kept separate.

The Academy is preparing this structured learning activity for independent CPD assessment. External certification, if granted, will apply only to the approved activity and authorised learning hours.

The Fairy Tails Training Academy will separately assess knowledge, practical delivery, case evidence and professional judgement. The programme is not presented as an Ofqual-regulated qualification, a government licence or independent professional accreditation of an individual trainer.

Current status
No CPD logo, provider number or certified-hours claim is being used. Public wording will change only after written authorisation.

Learning outcomes

What learners must be able to do

1

Explain the principal welfare duties affecting routine dog training and recognise when welfare, health or pain concerns require referral.

2

Observe and interpret common canine body-language indicators, including stress, fear, frustration, conflict and rising arousal.

3

Apply reinforcement-based learning principles and explain the consequences of pain, intimidation, flooding and forced proximity.

4

Plan a short humane session with clear criteria, suitable reinforcement, measurable observations and a realistic progression or regression.

5

Select and use routine leads, harnesses, long lines, barriers and other equipment safely and without causing avoidable distress.

6

Teach and demonstrate foundation skills including engagement, sit, down, settle, loose-lead walking, recall, drop and leave-it foundations.

7

Manage distance, environment and exposure so that the dog remains able to learn rather than being overwhelmed.

8

Coach a handler clearly, set proportionate home practice and produce objective session and risk records.

9

Recognise scope limits and refer appropriately where pain, medical concerns, serious aggression, severe distress or complex behaviour is suspected.

10

Reflect on performance, use feedback constructively and identify appropriate future CPD.

Assessment

Attendance alone is not enough

Knowledge

Forty controlled questions. Pass mark 80%, with every critical welfare and safety question correct.

Practical delivery

Observed dog handling and handler coaching. Every critical criterion must be competent, plus at least 75% of scored indicators.

Case portfolio

Two anonymised cases and at least six session records demonstrating planning, evidence, welfare and reflection.

Professional discussion

Structured questions test judgement, scope, referral, ethics and the ability to learn from evidence.

Non-compensatory safety standard
An unsafe or inhumane decision cannot be cancelled out by a high score elsewhere. Every component must be passed.

Independent quality assurance

External adjudication is built into the model.

  • All candidates sampled where the cohort has fewer than three learners
  • Otherwise at least 25% or three learners, whichever is greater
  • Every assessor and assessment component covered across the year
  • Mandatory review of serious welfare matters, conflicts and final appeals
  • Annual report with actions, owners and closure evidence

Governance

Published controls support fair decisions.

  • Welfare and pain-free training policy
  • Scope and veterinary or specialist referral policy
  • Equality and reasonable-adjustment procedure
  • Assessment, reassessment and appeal procedures
  • Complaints, malpractice and conflict controls
  • Data, confidentiality, certificate and marketing controls
