1. Provider and strategic route

The Fairy Tails K9 Centre is preparing one initial activity for independent CPD assessment. The smallest suitable provider route is requested: one course only, with the full written price, VAT, term, renewal, amendment and resubmission position confirmed before payment.

The route separates external certification of structured learning from the Academy’s separate assessment of practical competence. It does not create an Ofqual-regulated qualification, government licence or independent accreditation of an individual trainer.

3. Learning outcomes

  1. Explain the principal welfare duties affecting routine dog training and recognise when welfare, health or pain concerns require referral.
  2. Observe and interpret common canine body-language indicators, including stress, fear, frustration, conflict and rising arousal.
  3. Apply reinforcement-based learning principles and explain the consequences of pain, intimidation, flooding and forced proximity.
  4. Plan a short humane session with clear criteria, suitable reinforcement, measurable observations and a realistic progression or regression.
  5. Select and use routine leads, harnesses, long lines, barriers and other equipment safely and without causing avoidable distress.
  6. Teach and demonstrate foundation skills including engagement, sit, down, settle, loose-lead walking, recall, drop and leave-it foundations.
  7. Manage distance, environment and exposure so that the dog remains able to learn rather than being overwhelmed.
  8. Coach a handler clearly, set proportionate home practice and produce objective session and risk records.
  9. Recognise scope limits and refer appropriately where pain, medical concerns, serious aggression, severe distress or complex behaviour is suspected.
  10. Reflect on performance, use feedback constructively and identify appropriate future CPD.

5. Assessment

Knowledge requires 80% and every critical welfare or safety answer. Practical work requires all critical criteria and 75% of scored indicators. Two anonymised portfolio cases require at least six session records. Professional discussion tests reasoning, scope, referral and reflection.

Safety-critical and welfare-critical criteria are non-compensatory.

6. External adjudication

An independent adjudicator will review standards before delivery, sample all candidates in cohorts below three and otherwise sample at least 25% or three learners, whichever is greater. Serious welfare issues, conflicts, contested failures and final appeals receive mandatory independent review.

7. Policies

Welfare and humane-training policy

Positive reinforcement and environment-led management are the default. Pain-based, fear-based and intimidation-based methods are prohibited; work stops or changes where welfare is compromised.

Scope and referral policy

Learners do not diagnose illness or claim competence in complex clinical behaviour. Suspected pain or illness is referred to a veterinary surgeon; serious aggression, severe fear, severe separation distress and cases outside competence are referred to a suitably qualified professional.

Equality and reasonable-adjustment policy

Reasonable adjustments may alter access, format, timing or evidence method without weakening any safety-critical or competence standard.

Assessment and reassessment policy

Every component must pass and decisions must be evidence-based. Reassessment is normally limited to the failed component unless validity or safety requires wider reassessment.

Appeals and complaints

A learner may request an informal review and then a formal appeal. A person not involved in the original decision reviews the matter, with final-stage contested decisions available for external adjudication.

Malpractice, data and conflicts

Plagiarism, fabricated records, coached evidence, unsafe conduct, unmanaged conflicts and falsified attendance can lead to withheld results, investigation or withdrawal. Only necessary personal data is collected and evidence is anonymised wherever possible.

Certificate and marketing control

No CPD-branded certificate, logo, provider number or approved-hours claim is used until the activity is approved and the exact wording is authorised in writing.

8. Public wording

After approval, only the provider-authorised activity title, hours, logos and wording will be used. The Academy will clearly identify itself as the decision-maker for any separate practical-competence award.
